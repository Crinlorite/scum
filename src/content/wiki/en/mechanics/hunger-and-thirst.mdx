SCUM does not have a simple "hunger" bar. It tracks **calories, hydration, vitamins, minerals and macronutrients** (proteins, carbs, fats) separately.

## The basics

- **Calories**: your body burns them constantly. At 0, you lose HP and stamina.
- **Hydration**: drops faster than calories. At 0, you die quickly.

## What the UI hides

Eating only meat fills calories but leaves you without vitamins/minerals. In the medium term, your character **gets sick**, loses endurance and tires faster. The ideal mix includes:

- **Meat** (protein + fat)
- **Vegetables and fruit** (vitamins, minerals)
- **Bread/cooked grain** (fast carbs)

## Hunger states

| State | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Well fed | Slow stamina and HP regen. |
| Mild hunger | No noticeable penalty. |
| Strong hunger | You lose weight, max stamina drops. |
| Starvation | Continuous damage. |

Drink **boiled or purified water**. Drinking from puddles or rivers unfiltered gives you dysentery, which dehydrates you faster than you can drink.
