Welcome to the island. Your character spawns in a swimsuit with zero useful information. This guide is the bare minimum you need.

## First 5 minutes

1. **Open the map (M)** and find the nearest village. Avoid marked military zones until you know what you are doing.
2. **Drink water.** Every village has fountains. Thirst rises fast and kills.
3. **Eat something.** Chickens, rabbits and tree apples will do. Without food, your HP drops.
4. **Get a backpack.** Without it, you cannot carry anything useful.

## What to avoid on day one

- **Puppets** (the infected) in groups. One can be killed with a stick; three will kill you.
- **Military zones.** The loot is brutal but mechs see you before you see them.
- **PvP in coastal villages.** Everyone spawns there and the mortality rate is high.

## Next step

Once you have food, water, a backpack and a decent melee weapon, check the [mechanics](/en/wiki/mechanics) section to understand why your character does weird things.
